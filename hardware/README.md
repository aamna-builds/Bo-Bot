# Hardware (mechanical build)

The robot is built from stacked plates:

- **Base plate:** laser-cut acrylic, 89 x 62 mm, with the two N20 motors held by 3D-printed clamps
  ([`../cad/laser-cut/base_plate/`](../cad/laser-cut/base_plate/), [`../cad/printed-parts/n20_clamp/`](../cad/printed-parts/n20_clamp/)).
- **Plates and stands:** the plates are held by 3D-printed spacers / pins and the yellow plate stands
  ([`../cad/printed-parts/`](../cad/printed-parts/)). The LiPo sits low between the plates to keep the centre of mass near the axle.
- **Top:** Arduino Uno, with the MPU-6050 and DRV8833 on a mini breadboard.
- **Front:** 3D-printed faceplate with the OLED window and the BO-BOT name.

A thermocol casing was the first prototype. The first assembly checklist is in the [assembly and first-test guide](assembly-guide.md).
