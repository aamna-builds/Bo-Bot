Standoff pins v3 (4 mm plates, 17 mm gap)
1. Print pin_SIZE_TEST.stl: 4 short pins. Shortest = 3.4 mm peg, then 3.7, 4.0, longest = 4.4 mm.
2. Push each into a plate hole. Pick the THICKEST one that still goes in and clicks.
3. Print the matching pin_set_peg_X.Xmm.stl (6 pins).
PLA/PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 100 % infill, no supports, lying on the side as laid out.
None fits? Measure the hole and set peg_d = hole - 0.1 in plate_pin.scad.
